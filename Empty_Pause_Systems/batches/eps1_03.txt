======================================================================================================================
Model: preemptions arrive as a Poisson process at rate lambda per hour of running (work or checkpoint), never during a restart R;
every wall hour on spot capacity is billed at the spot price; the job's final output write is common to all arms and excluded.
WALL: exact renewal E[T] = (e^{lambda W} - 1)(1/lambda + R). H0: exact segment renewal at tau* = sqrt(2 delta/lambda),
n-1 segments of tau + delta and a last segment of W - (n-1) tau with no checkpoint; a preemption during a checkpoint loses
the segment. ETM: E[T] = W + lambda W R (a notice save loses nothing). Stake = expected extra hours per preemption =
(E[T] - T_no-preemption) / E[preemptions]; lost work = stake - R. Cost = spot price x E[T]; on-demand bill = W x 1.
