CHOICES
  CHOICE Poisson offers: arrivals at rate lambda per wall hour; at most one event per slot of 24 h (further arrivals in the slot
         are void; the event starts at the slot's start), so each slot carries an offer with p = 1 - exp(-lambda slot), independently;
         p is the best rational with denominator <= 10^6 and its rounding error is printed. The fleet knows lambda, not the count.
  CHOICE Slots at hours 24 k strictly before hour W = 1000 (EPS2's offer window: every arm is running at every slot).
  CHOICE Each arm decides per offer by exact backward induction on its valuation plus payments (ties accept), the rest of the
         season played optimally at the same flat pi; x* (the minimum acceptable payment) is the start of the final acceptance
         interval in pi at the state, computed exactly from the convex piecewise-linear value functions (drlib.xstar_all).
  CHOICE 'At every offer' = at every reachable offer state (k, n, L) of every scored cell; Q-a needs
         x* == rate (R + S)/H there with an acceptance region [x*, inf) (closed). (R + S)/H is R/H when S = 0: the save time
         counts as own hours without progress, added to R (drlib's CHOICE).
  CHOICE 'The slack' = the arm's remaining deadline budget before the offer, D - W - (wall delay so far) for WALL and OWN,
         D - W - n (R + S) for ETM. 'Rise as the slack falls' (reading B, scored) = for OWN and for WALL, in every cell: among
         the states that can still meet the deadline (slack >= 0), at each offer x* never falls as the slack falls, and at every
         offer where a zero-slack state (one more event misses the deadline) is reachable, x* there is strictly above x* at
         full slack (n = 0); at least one scored cell must reach a zero-slack state, else Q-b is not computable. States past
         the deadline (slack < 0: the job is already lost) are not part of 'as the slack falls'; they are counted and printed.
         Reading A (printed, not scored) also requires a zero-slack state to be reachable in every cell.
  CHOICE Scored grid: EPS2 T1's cells (D x H) x the rates x the two overhead settings above. Sensitivity (printed,
         not scored): slots of 12 and 48 h at lambda_DFS; a boundary cell where ETM's own-step slack is smaller than the
         season's possible overheads (D = 1002 h).
  CHOICE H0 (an interruptible-load contract covering every made offer) is printed for context (its reservation price = expected
         opportunity cost / expected curtailed hours); TIER is not part of H1 (H2 checks it).
  CHOICE G-POS per (cell, pi): OWN's or WALL's expected accepted events strictly below ETM's (exact rationals). G-ZERO: ETM's
         one-event stake minus rate (R + S) at every event count n = 0..K-1 of every scored cell, exactly 0. G-NEG: with no events
         offered (K = 0, and each cell's realisation with no arrival), ETM not ahead of WALL by more than 1 % (harness rel) on
         any outcome: curtailed fleet-hours, payments, valuation, fleet value, deadline met, completion hour (earlier is ahead).
