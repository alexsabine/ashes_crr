  CHOICE Resume power models (all three scored): M-step: a released job draws full power at once (its restart R runs at full
         power, own hours without progress); M-idle: it draws 0 during the restart R and full power after it; M-ramp: from its
         release its power rises at the sourced 1.9 p.u. per second (LLTF-ramp) to full power. A lossless pause
         returns each job to its own full draw; its deferred work extends the job in time (no catch-up capacity, no demand
         above the pre-event baseline in the model).
  CHOICE 'The rebound peak' (scored) = the largest load increase within any 1-minute window after the event (the window of the
         sourced MW/min limits); 'the curtailed load' = the pre-event load minus the event-level load. Q-a (scored): in every
         cell (resume model x H) the simultaneous resume's rebound peak equals the curtailed load exactly AND the post-event
         maximum minus the event level equals the curtailed load exactly (the return is a step to the baseline, not above it).
         Rises within 1 s and 10 s windows and the peak above the baseline are printed.
  CHOICE 'A staggered resume' (scored) = S1, batch release: b = floor(L x 1 min / P) jobs per minute, rank r released at
         floor(r / b) minutes. Any release schedule whose rise within every 1-minute window is <= b P releases rank r no earlier
         than floor(r / b) minutes (b + 1 releases cannot share a half-open 1-minute window), so S1 gives every rank its least
         delay under the limit. 'Removes it' (Q-b, scored) = in every cell (resume model x H x ramp limit) where the simultaneous
         peak exceeds the limit L (binding), b >= 1 (a feasible stagger), the staggered rebound peak <= L and < the simultaneous
         peak. 'At a cost' (scored) = the stagger defers job time: the summed release delay over the jobs is > 0. Non-binding
         limits need no stagger (b >= N) and are printed, not scored. The alternative reading 'a cost in ETM's own valuation'
         is computed and printed, not scored.
  CHOICE S2 (printed, not scored): every job resumes at once under a fleet-wide power cap rising at exactly L MW per minute
         (the NV-powercap quote); throughput = power fraction (linear curve, ASSUMED, drlib's default), so each job loses T/2
         hours of progress, T = N P / L minutes; its cost is computed as a stagger of T/2 for every job.
  CHOICE Stagger order (printed, not scored): tight-first (ranks by slack, tightest released first) and loose-first, the same
