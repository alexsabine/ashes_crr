CHOICES
  CHOICE A product with response time T is met only if the fleet's power is down by T after the operator's instruction (or
         the trigger); a later drop is a non-delivery, not a slower delivery. The save starts at the instruction: the fleet
         does not save pre-emptively before it knows of the event (a pre-emptive save is the periodic checkpoint below).
  CHOICE The lossless route (ETM as declared): save for S at full power, then the power drops. It meets the product iff
         S <= T (drlib.response_ok, ties meet). A save is atomic: an interrupted save leaves only the last complete checkpoint.
  CHOICE The lossy route: the power drops at the instruction without a save (drlib.lossy): the job loses the work since
         its last periodic checkpoint, I/2 in expectation (the instruction's phase uniform over the interval I), redone as
         own hours without progress; S = 0. Stopping the computation is taken as instantaneous, so this route meets every
         product. These two routes are the only ways a pause meets a product; declining the event does not meet it.
  CHOICE 'ETM cannot meet the product without losing work' (per cell with S > T) = the lossless route does not meet T AND
         every route that meets T loses work (the least lost work among the meeting routes > 0) AND the pause's stake
         beyond the restart on that route is > 0 at every event count n = 0..K-1 (ETM's stake - rate R, drlib.stake).
  CHOICE Q holds iff that reading holds in every scored cell (product x save x restart x interval) with S > T; Q is not
         computable if any sourced quote is not found or not parsed, if any p_all is not a threshold, or if no scored cell
         has S > T (Q would be vacuous). The converse (S <= T: the lossless route meets with no lost work) is printed.
  CHOICE The feasible product set = for each save S, the scored products with S <= T (the lossless route meets them).
  CHOICE Prices (context for Q, not scored): p_all per curtailed fleet-hour, EPS2's decisive reading (drlib.p_all), for
         ETM and OWN, one offer every 24 wall hours before hour W (K = 41), known in advance; verified by backward
         induction at p_all and p_all - 1e-6. The lossless route's overhead is R + S, the lossy route's R + I/2.
  CHOICE DFS (no minimum notice, a 24 h maximum lead) has no stated T: printed at its two bounds, not scored and counted as
         an exclusion. F4's cloud pre-emption notices and power-control latencies are printed as context, not scored.
  CHOICE G-ZERO: on the lossless route, ETM's one-event stake minus rate (R + S) is exactly 0 at every n = 0..K-1 and
         ETM's x* == rate (R + S)/H (closed) at every offer state, for every (restart, save) pair (as H1 and H4: the save
         counts with R). The stake minus rate R alone (the declaration's wording 'beyond R') is printed; it equals rate S.
  CHOICE G-NEG: with no events offered (K = 0, and the no-offer realisation), ETM not ahead of WALL by more than 1 %
         (harness rel) on any outcome (curtailed fleet-hours, payments, valuation, fleet value, deadline met, completion hour,
         earlier ahead), on every lossless and lossy job, at pi = ETM's p_all of the job.
  NOTE   Q follows from the model's definitions once S > T (a save that has not finished leaves only the last periodic
         checkpoint); the check's content is the arithmetic, the feasible product set and the price of the lossy fallback.
