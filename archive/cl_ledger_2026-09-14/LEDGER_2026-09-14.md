## 1. Learner unit (PREREG_unit.md) — FAIL as hoped-for, informative
KL-based estimator-free Fisher speed does not collapse the four EMA-rate landscapes (optima Ω = 4/8/16 on MNIST/KMNIST/
Fashion). Implied optimal EMA rate is ~constant per update (0.023–0.032) while Fisher speed varies 3×: the invariant was
the experimenter's clock. CIFAR arm not completed (download failures).

## 2. Compute claim as pitched (PREREG_compute.md) — FAIL at both scales
CRR-CLS was always run at 100 % replay; at 5 % replay it reaches 37 % vs 56 % for 50 % uniform replay (class-IL), and
costs more compute (teacher forward ≥ 1/3 of a step). LM regime: EMA teacher preserves the past only by not learning
(Ω = 64 → domain bpb 4.29); every EMA arm Pareto-dominated by some replay arm. CRR rates = tuned fixed rates (L3 pass).

## 3. Equanimity replay (PREREG_equanimity.md, estimator finalised post hoc on KMNIST seed 0; all else held out)
w = Ω·‖ḡ_present‖/‖ḡ_past‖ on EMA-smoothed mean gradients; Ω = 1.
Ω = 1 is the sharp interior optimum in 6/6 landscapes (MNIST, KMNIST, Fashion × r = 0.1, 0.2), ±√2 costs 5–45 pts.
r = 0.2 matches 100 % replay on KMNIST (62.5 vs 61.1) and MNIST (80.6 vs 79.9) at 40 % less compute; Fashion-MNIST
does not (66.9 vs 74.7; equal-compute +1.0). Recurring streams: same pattern.

## 5. SOTA-facing (PREREG_sota.md)
T1 PASS — path length beats endpoint KL as a forgetting predictor: R² 0.887 (C_new) / 0.901 (C_old) vs 0.756
(endpoint KL on new task, RL's Razor's predictor), n = 20 runs; pass line +0.05. Endpoint saturates at high lr while
forgetting and path length keep rising. Surplus S/C* = 2–20: SGD trajectories are mostly wasted travel.
T2 — equanimity vs Marek et al.'s KL-to-base recipe on the 4-domain stream (2 seeds):
  EQ(0.25)  D0 2.439  dom 3.311  compute 3.75   |  ER(0.25) 2.607/3.371 @3.75  |  ER(0.5) 2.461/3.252 @4.51
  KLrep λ=1 2.462/3.617 @4.00 · λ=3 2.408/3.765 · λ=10 2.377/3.986
  EQ dominates ER(0.25) on both axes at equal compute, ≈ ER(0.5) at 17 % less, and dominates KL-to-base at every λ on
  the plasticity axis (anchoring to the pretrained origin blocks learning of earlier domains in a multi-domain stream).
  Pre-registered 0.03 forgetting margin vs λ = 10 not met (0.062) — but that arm has dom 3.99, off the frontier.
T3 FAIL — abundant memory (buffer 500 → 5000): ER last-task 87.7 → 85.1 (Cho effect reproduced); EQ 83.2 → 79.5.
  Adaptive weight does not relax as the past saturates; with abundant memory, full replay is best (66.8).

## Standing claims after today
- Ω = 1 as a unit-free ratio (equal mean-gradient pull of settled past and present) is a sharp, lr/batch-invariant
  optimum on three datasets; a constant weight 1 does as well as the adaptive rule; Fisher vs Euclidean immaterial so far.
- Forgetting tracks Fisher path length, not endpoint displacement (one toy LM, n = 20). Untested elsewhere; RL's Razor's
  public data is the next test.
- Retracted: 30–50 % compute via EMA self-distillation; "no free parameters" for the EMA rate; equanimity as a
  plasticity fix.
