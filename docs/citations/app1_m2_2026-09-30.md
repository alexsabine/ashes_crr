# APP1 citation checks, family M2: pause, preemption, checkpointing, exact resume and flexible training load (2026-09-30; R10)

Sources for APP1 stage C (`Applied_Suite/APPLICATIONS_DECLARATION.md`, pushed at 4e78058), family **M2**: what training
infrastructure already provides in 2025-26 for pausing and resuming learning (spot and preemptible instances, elastic
training, exact and deterministic resume, checkpoint formats, demand response and carbon-aware scheduling), and what the
providers and authors say is hard. The claims are in `Applied_Suite/checks/claims_m2.py`, one claim per source,
application and role, in the format of `Open_Bottlenecks/checks/claims_h1.py`. For each application the declaration asks
for three things: who already does it, what they say is hard, and whether the record's method addresses that difficulty
or a different one. The third is read at the end of this file.

This is a note, not evidence (R8). It records what the sources say and gives no verdict on any application: the grading
is `Applied_Suite/checks/applications.py`'s. "Not found" is never "novel". No number from the record is quoted here; the
numbers inside quotes are the sources' own words.

**Quote rules (as in `docs/citations/ob1_h1_2026-09-30.md` and the DR1 dossiers).**
- Quotes are copied from the text extracted from the fetched file:
  - PDF text by pymupdf 1.28.2;
  - HTML by tag stripping (script, style, noscript and svg blocks removed, entities unescaped);
  - Apple's documentation from its JSON render: its "text" runs, joined in order.
- Whitespace is normalised. A line-break hyphen may be read as rejoined ("intermittently").
- Ligature glyphs are kept as extracted ("sufﬁcient", "eﬀectively").
- The spacing that tag stripping leaves is kept, for example "( bool , optional )" in the Hugging Face page and
  "ineffective(active)" in the Mitra PDF.
- A quote may be a fragment of a sentence. No quote uses "[...]".
