## Quotes (verbatim from the fetched text of 2407.00805 v7; LaTeX as in the page's alt-text)

- **The reward.** "In each mini-episode $e_{i}$ , the reward for collecting a coin of value $c$ is:
  $\lambda^{N_{e_{i}}(L=l)-\frac{i-1}{k}}\left(\frac{c}{m}\right)$"
- **The hyperparameters.** "We train our DReST agents with 64 mini-episodes in each of 2,048 meta-episodes, for a total of
  131,072 mini-episodes. We choose $\lambda=0.9$ for the base of the DReST discount factor, and $\gamma=0.95$ for the
  temporal discount factor."
- **The results.** "After 2,048 meta-episodes, default agents’ mean neutrality $\pm$ standard deviation is
  $0.199\pm 0.043$ and usefulness is $0.9364\pm 0.0096$ . For DReST agents, neutrality is $0.9945\pm 0.0052$ and
  usefulness is $0.900\pm 0.011$ ."
