## Quotes (verbatim from the fetched text; LaTeX as rendered in the page's alt-text)

- **The update.** "We quantify the magnitude of model change at step $t$ using the parameter update norm
  $\Delta_{t}=\|\Theta_{t}-\Theta_{t-1}\|_{2},$"
- **What Δ covers.** "In practice, $\Delta_{t}$ is computed over trainable parameters only (i.e., the LoRA Hu et al. (2021)
  weights)"
- **Model time.** "Accumulating these updates yields a model-centric notion of time, $\tau_{t}=\sum_{i=1}^{t}\Delta_{i},$
  (3) which represents the total distance the model has traveled in parameter space."
- **The reset.** "In our implementation, $\tau_{t}$ is reset at the beginning of each new task and measures the elapsed
  model-centric time since learning on the current task started."
- **The claim for model time.** "Unlike raw step counts, $\tau_{t}$ directly reflects model evolution and is substantially
  less sensitive to optimization hyperparameters."
- **The model day.** "we define a virtual model “day” as the amount of parameter change observed over an initial window
  of $S$ training steps"
- **The replay weight.** "$\beta_{t}=\beta_{\text{base}}\cdot\mathrm{clip}\!\left(1+\gamma(r_{t}-1),\,g_{\min},\,g_{\max}\right),$"
- **The replay loss.** "$\mathcal{L}_{\text{replay}}=\mathcal{L}_{\text{task}}^{(\text{old})}+\beta_{t}\sum_{j}\|\Theta_{j}-\Theta_{j}^{\star}\|_{2}^{2},$"
- **The constants.**
  - "The smoothing coefficient $\lambda$ in Eq. ( 8 ) is fixed to 0.05."
  - "The base regularization coefficient $\beta_{\text{base}}$ in Eq. ( 10 ) is set to $10^{-3}$ , with clipping bounds
    $g_{\min}=0.5$ and $g_{\max}=3.0$ ."
  - γ's value is not stated in the text read.
- **Why β_base matters.** "This calibration is critical: without proper scaling, the replay objective would be dominated
  by either the task loss or the regularization term, preventing effective control of replay strength."
- **The step-calibration ablation.** "Model-centric calibration consistently outperforms step-based calibration, yielding
  an average improvement of 1.2% in OP and 1.1% in BWT."
- **The forgetting dynamics.** "The early-stage forgetting rate is approximately 7 $\times$ higher than that in later
  stages, indicating strongly non-linear dynamics."
