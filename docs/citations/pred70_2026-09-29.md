  batch used as the domain value.
- **The paper also tunes the effective rate.** It tunes η_eff = η/(1 − β), so the dampened update (the A6 server) is
  FedAvgM at a smaller effective rate. That is inside the family the paper tunes.
- **What this does to the label.** The row's ADDS holds against FedAvgM at the paper's fixed server rate, not against a
  tuned FedAvgM.
