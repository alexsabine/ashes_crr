**contradicts** (asserts or shows the opposite, in whole or in part). The reading policy is RQM's, unchanged; it and the
operational reading of each position are in the docstring of `Relational_Reference_Memory/checks/claims_w1.py`:

  difficulty of exemplar-free or small-memory class-IL in a setting trained from scratch.
- **P2** is read per the caller's instruction for this family: **states** needs a same-protocol comparison in the paper
  itself, from scratch, against a replay method with a stated realistic buffer (2000 on a 100-class set, or 20 per class),

  replay. The agent's operationalisation, for review: "level" is within about one point or ahead on the main from-scratch
  setting(s); "clearly behind" is more than about two points behind the best replay row on most settings. A smaller or
  unstated buffer makes **close**; exemplar-free arms that are replay methods with the buffer removed, or a table mixed
  across settings, make **bears**.
