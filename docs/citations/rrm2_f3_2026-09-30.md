## A. Anchors and relative representations inside one continual learner (P4)

### 1. CAMELS, continual anchored manifold embeddings (Ranganathan et al., CoLLAs 2025 Workshop Track)

- **Identifier:** OpenReview forum bqievBMLYE (https://openreview.net/forum?id=bqievBMLYE), venue "CoLLAs 2025 - Workshop
  Track" (venue id lifelong-ml.cc/CoLLAs/2025/Workshop_Track), note created 23 May 2025, published 12 Jun 2025, modified
  3 Aug 2025 (from the API record), fetched 2026-09-30. Kailash Ranganathan, Arnav Dalal, Arjun Banerjee. Not found on arXiv.
- **Integrity:** full text **NOT REACHED** (openreview.net PDF: HTTP 403). Abstract from the OpenReview search API response
  `search/or_relative+representations+continual+learning.json` sha256
  2bfdb94b6a24cbaad26f3f2ded4c1316c2309f92638e249118d2562a0d8332c5; abstract text file `or_bqievBMLYE_abstract.txt` sha256
  450e0070f4ec8289d1d30f28925783040ec00a68983c903e7a90921926b4e21d.
- **Mechanism (from the abstract only):** a continual learner that constrains its latent space to preserve the pairwise cosine
  similarities among replay samples (relative geometry) rather than their coordinates, with tasks in orthogonal subspaces.

> Rather than constraining parameters or matching global prototypes, CAMELS anchors the internal structure of past tasks by preserving pairwise cosine similarities among replay samples—maintaining relative geometry without freezing coordinates and embeds different tasks in orthogonal subspaces.

> This formulation treats continual learning as the problem of preserving local isometries across evolving latent manifolds in high-dimensional embedding spaces.

- **Reading.** P4 **states** (from the abstract): relative geometry among kept samples is held inside one continual learner
  across its own updates. It is a relational constraint during training (close to the relational distillation graded N4 in
  `docs/citations/rrm_2026-09-30.md`), not content stored as coordinates relative to anchors and rebuilt from them. (Claim w3:0.)

### 2. Transport keys (Chaudhury, arXiv technical report 2026)

- **Identifier:** arXiv:2606.02860, current version **v1** (1 Jun 2026; the only version listed), comment "Technical report
  showcasing results from transport keys", fetched 2026-09-30. Archie Chaudhury.
- **Integrity:** PDF (v1) `pdf_2606.02860v1.pdf` sha256 c0fb60558d14a61f3dd90851029c2b86586f46bdd96cda4cfae4757a00f9fbf3;
  abstract page sha256 195ecabd7934699ff517d23ac13a5951fdb3dc3577369e63307d2e2403061983.
- **Mechanism:** after sequential training, early stages of the post-update network are stitched to late stages of its
  predecessor through a small alignment operator ("transport key") fitted on paired activations of a few kept earlier-task
  examples passed through both checkpoints.

> We describe transport keys at a systems level as compact interface-alignment operators estimated from a small set of paired anchor activations and evaluated through model stitching.

> To estimate a key, we use a small anchor set drawn from Task A. Anchors are ordinary examples from the earlier task and are passed through both checkpoints.

- **Reading.** P4 **states**: anchor-fitted latent alignment inside one continual learner, across its own update, to recover
  the use of old representations. It keeps the predecessor's late stages (a stored old checkpoint) and does not transport stored
  class statistics. It also bears on F2's P5 (a map fitted on a few **kept** old-task anchors, applied across one learner's
  update), for the F2 dossier's reading. Single-author technical report. (Claim w3:1.)

### 3. LAVA, language-anchored visual alignment (Geng et al., arXiv 2025)

- **Identifier:** arXiv:2511.14401, current version **v1** (18 Nov 2025; the only version listed), no comment or journal
  reference, fetched 2026-09-30. Shuyi Geng, Tao Zhou, Yi Zhou.
- **Integrity:** PDF (v1) `pdf_2511.14401v1.pdf` sha256 3c23243b822583d20e1b1812415ec8f665f4f1920c2a75d7a5e538080cbf8a10;
  abstract page sha256 eccd26bab2f2cbcfc65733dc06cafbd8d78913ccd24b15613dcf584bb5327d05.
- **Mechanism:** domain-incremental learning on a frozen visual encoder with per-domain prompts; each domain's features are
  encoded by cosine similarity to learnable visual anchors and aligned (KL) to the fixed relative geometry of class-name text
  anchors, so that domains share one relative space; it cites Moschella et al. and Maiorca et al.

> we propose LAVA (Language-Anchored Visual Alignment), a novel DIL framework that replaces direct feature alignment with relative alignment driven by a text-based reference anchor. LAVA guides the visual representations of each incoming domain to preserve a consistent relative geometry, which is defined by mirroring the pairwise semantic similarities between the class names.

> Once the Visual Anchors are structurally aligned with the Text-based Anchor, the resulting visual geometry space becomes comparable across domains. We exploit this relative representation to facilitate effective class-wise aggregation of cross-domain features.

> we keep the visual encoder fθ frozen and maintain a pool of prompts

- **Reading.** P4 **close**: relative representations used inside one incremental learner, but to make domains comparable on a
  frozen backbone, not to keep old features valid across the learner's own weight updates. (Claim w3:2.)
