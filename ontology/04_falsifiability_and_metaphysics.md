# 04 — What making CRR falsifiable has done to its metaphysics

The framing is Popper's (1934/1959) and Lakatos's (1970), named as context and not fetched: a
metaphysics becomes science at the point where it forbids something, and a research programme is
judged by whether its protective belt of auxiliary assumptions is adding predictions or absorbing
refutations. Every number is a pinned row; each section ends with a fifth-grader line.

## 1. What falsifiability provided, in six results

**1. A metaphysics with a failure map.** Seventeen synthesis rows and three ledger studies are
definite refutations of a CRR clause read as a rule (file 03). Process metaphysics in Whitehead's
line has not previously had a list of the systems on which it is false. That is the first thing
falsifiability gave: the commitments were stated clearly enough to be wrong, and they were, in
Bayesian statistics, thermodynamics, neuroscience, ecology and cosmology.

**2. A separation of the borrowed from the owned.** The synthesis class split CRR into what it shares
with information geometry and what is its own, and found that the shared part accounts for all of
the breadth (34 rows REDUNDANT-IG, 50 REDUNDANT-DOMAIN). Metaphysically this is the most important
result: the universality of "change is distinguishability" is the universality of a theorem, and
carries no ontological weight beyond the fact that the systems admit statistical descriptions.

**3. A tense result.** The open-future commitment (A7, A8) was converted into an operational demand
on an instrument, and the instrument failed it by a measured amount (0.146 half-turns; two half-turns
of future to settle; batch 21 row 2). The repair (a causal phase) empties H-CUT on one-dimensional
traces (batch 07 row 3). So a thesis about the reality of the future became a trade-off between two
of CRR's own clauses, with numbers on both sides. No ontology of time has been given a number like
that before in this repository, and it is the kind of number the theory can now be held to.

**4. A discreteness result.** Nothing in 335 rows found becoming to be discrete. The occasions that
needed no antipode were the systems' own events; the resolution ρ was reported everywhere and never
found to be a property of a carrier; on the one truly discrete carrier the instrument returned its own
detrender (batch 26 row 2). The metaphysics of occasions is, so far, a way of segmenting continuous
dynamics, not a discovery that dynamics comes segmented.

**5. A memory result.** "Re-weighting, never accumulation" (A6) is false wherever the domain's
mathematics accumulates, and where it is integrated with a non-accumulating rule it reduces to an
exponential kernel (the two candidates) that cannot produce a memory effect the domain has (Kovacs,
batch 19 row 3). The process account of memory, as CRR states it, is either the domain's forgetting
factor or wrong.

**6. A unit result.** "The system's own unit" (A1′) turned out to be several estimators that disagree
by measured factors (0.8629 by leverage; a factor of 2.8284 between window readings on a Poisson
carrier, batch 01 row 3; 4 of 5 densities on a quantile reading, batch 23 row 2). A metaphysics of
intrinsic measure needs one intrinsic measure.

> Six things we learned by making CRR bet: it can lose (and did, seventeen times); its big
> agreements were borrowed; its idea that the future is blank got a number and its own machine broke
> it; nothing showed time comes in chunks; its idea of memory was wrong where things add up; and "the
> thing's own ruler" turned out to be several rulers.

## 2. What remains outside any test

Three commitments cannot be reached by this pipeline or any like it, and it is fair to say so.

- **That the future has no content (A8 as ontology).** No computation can distinguish a contentless
  future from a determined one whose content is unknown; only the operational demand (A7 on an
  instrument) is testable, and that is what was tested.
- **That the cut is real (A3 as ontology).** Whether the world has Nows or only our instruments do is
  not a question a pinned output answers. What is answerable is whether one rule for the cut exists
  and whether it beats the extremum, and both are currently open or negative.
- **That distinguishability is what change is (C1).** As a definition it cannot fail; as a claim about
  the world it does not say what would count against it.

Lakatos would say the programme is progressive only if its auxiliary choices (which phase, which
unit, which normalisation of P3) are made in advance and then predict something. Every one of those
choices is currently made after the fact, row by row, and that is the mark of a programme absorbing
refutations rather than issuing predictions. The v3.2 decision list (file 05) is the programme's
chance to reverse that.

> Some of CRR's ideas cannot be tested at all, like "the future is blank" as a fact about the world.
> That is allowed, but then it is philosophy, not science, and we should say which is which.

## 3. What the metaphysics looks like after the pipeline

Stated as plainly as the results allow:

- The world admits Fisher geometry wherever it admits statistics. (Theorem.)
- Systems with their own events keep an arc between events that is the geometry of a monotone
  occasion, and its regularity is the domain's rule (the adder principle, the reservoir rule, the
  clamp). (Class map, computed.)
- A Now located by an intrinsic phase is not, in general, locatable from the settled past; where it
  is, it is the system's own event or a section, and no antipode is involved. (Instrument result.)
- Memory that re-weights rather than counts is the domain's forgetting factor where it applies and
  false where the domain counts. (Refutation.)
- Whether a CRR quantity ever beats a conventional one on unseen data remains open, with one fragile
  provisional pass. (Ledger.)

That is a smaller metaphysics than CRR.md states, and a more honest one. It retains the process
picture as a picture (file 02 says whose pictures they mostly are), and it retains one live bet.

> After all the tests, what is left is: distances measured by telling-apart work everywhere; some
> things really do keep their own count of change, for reasons those things already knew; the "now"
> line has to be drawn a different way; memory-as-blending is wrong where things keep score; and
> CRR's one real bet is still open.
