The cut on a machine: five facts (ontology/06_cut_as_content.md). Deterministic; every number the folder quotes about grids, floats, deltas, resets and non-local phase is printed here.

[1] A point on a grid is a cell: the measure of one sample is 1/n of the record, never zero
     n =    100 samples: measure of one sample = 1.00e-02 of the record; a cut placed at a sample owns that share
     n =   1000 samples: measure of one sample = 1.00e-03 of the record; a cut placed at a sample owns that share
     n = 100000 samples: measure of one sample = 1.00e-05 of the record; a cut placed at a sample owns that share

[2] A Dedekind cut has no member of its own, yet it is the number; on a machine the cut has a width
     rationals with denominator <=   10: nearest below sqrt 2 = 7/5 = 1.400000, nearest above = 10/7 = 1.428571, gap 2.86e-02; no rational squares to 2 (the cut is empty of rationals)
     rationals with denominator <=  100: nearest below sqrt 2 = 140/99 = 1.414141, nearest above = 99/70 = 1.414286, gap 1.44e-04; no rational squares to 2 (the cut is empty of rationals)
     rationals with denominator <= 1000: nearest below sqrt 2 = 1393/985 = 1.414213, nearest above = 577/408 = 1.414216, gap 2.49e-06; no rational squares to 2 (the cut is empty of rationals)
     float64: spacing (ulp) at 1.0 = 2.220e-16, at sqrt 2 = 2.220e-16, at 1e6 = 1.164e-10: the finest cut a float can make has this width, and sqrt 2 in float64 is 1.4142135623730951 exactly, a rational

[3] A delta has zero support and unit mass: on a grid it is one cell of height 1/dx, and it still integrates to 1
     n =    10: cell width 0.2000, delta height 5.0, integral (rectangle rule) = 1.000000, support = one cell of width 0.2000
     n =   100: cell width 0.0200, delta height 50.0, integral (rectangle rule) = 1.000000, support = one cell of width 0.0200
     n =  1000: cell width 0.0020, delta height 500.0, integral (rectangle rule) = 1.000000, support = one cell of width 0.0020

[4] A reset located at the cut is a finite displacement with no duration: counted as arc it is content, and a constant content lowers CV(arc) by arithmetic
     2000 occasions, rise ~ N(1, 0.2), reset jump 1 at every cut: CV(rise) = 0.2012; CV(rise + jump) = 0.1003; ratio 0.4986 (closed form 1/(1 + jump/mean rise) = 0.4986)
     with a jump that varies like the rise (independent, same CV): CV(rise + jump) = 0.1404 (a constant at the cut regularises; a variable one does not)
