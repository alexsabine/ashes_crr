# Study T1x2 — path length against endpoint as a predictor of forgetting, learning rate controlled: FAIL on 5/5, not fragile

**T1X2-1 FAILS on every scored carrier.** On five unseen PMLB regression carriers, fine-tuning a numpy MLP (model (d)) in
108 runs per carrier, the endpoint KL on the old probe (E_old) predicts forgetting on held-out runs. Its R² is 0.693, 0.505,
0.624, 0.686 and 0.815. The best path predictor reaches 0.030, 0.352, −0.004, 0.001 and 0.073.

On the prereg's scale the path is behind the endpoint by 0.153 to 0.743 in R². That is far beyond the ±0.05 band, on
every carrier and in all 45 sensitivity cells (0 flips).

The fixed-learning-rate arm (T1X2-2) fails on 5/5. At lr 0.01 the Spearman correlation of the old-probe path with
forgetting is +0.003 to +0.582, against +0.915 to +0.983 for the old-probe endpoint.

- `runs/t1x2/rerun_check.txt` (every carrier's rerun byte-identical);

## What the rows say together

1. **H-T1 is retired as tested on this learner.** The prereg states what a FAIL means: "the endpoint is sufficient on this
   learner too and H-T1 retires as tested." The learning rate was controlled as a covariate, the path varied 5× or more
   within each learning rate, and the old-probe endpoint was in the race. The endpoint won on every carrier by a wide
   margin.
2. **This is the same answer ARC-T1b gave on the archived toy LM.** There the endpoint on the old probe reached R² 0.99 and
   the path did not beat it. It also matches the convex surrogate S-H, where the endpoint is a sufficient statistic by the
   lemma in `theory/checks/verify_scope_math.py`. On a non-convex learner (a one-hidden-layer MLP) that lemma does not
   apply, and the endpoint was still sufficient in practice.
3. **The surplus diagnostic is the only report-level trace of path dependence.** On `feynman_II_36_38` and `feynman_I_9_18`
   the runs with a large surplus S sit further from the endpoint fit (Spearman +0.485 and +0.659). This is report-only
   (T1X2-D). By CLAUDE.md §10 it is not a reinterpretation of the FAIL, and a hypothesis about it would be a new prereg.
4. **The consequence for continual learning.** Forgetting on these learners is predicted by how far the model's outputs on
   old data have moved, not by how far it travelled to get there. A method that limits forgetting should therefore bound
   the old-probe endpoint (distillation-style or KL-to-reference penalties), not the path. That is the mainstream reading
   (RL's Razor, `docs/citations/continual_learning_2026-09-22.md`), and this study agrees with it.

## What a surrogate would have done

`prereg/t1x2/gate_T1.txt` (unchanged since T1x): on the convex learner S-H the old-probe endpoint reaches R² 0.997 and the
path trails by 0.904. On the wear learner S-H2, built so that forgetting depends on the path, the path leads by +0.231. The
real carriers behave like S-H, not S-H2. The gate shows that the instrument can see path dependence when it is there. On
these learners it is not there.
